## 6. Risk limits, and what a breach triggers

| Trigger | Action | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Portfolio drawdown of 8% | Full portfolio review, written up | 72 hours |
| Single position down 15% | Written post-mortem; add, trim, or exit | 5 business days |
| Position unreviewed for 90 days | Re-test the three key assumptions | — |

A trigger is not a stop-loss. It is a forced question: at an 8% drawdown the thing to establish is not whether to sell, but whether the market is wrong or I am.

## 7. Attribution

Performance is decomposed quarterly into four buckets:

1. **Issuer selection** — did I pick the right names
2. **Capital structure tier** — was I in the right part of the stack
3. **Duration and curve** — what did positioning contribute
4. **Beta** — how much of it was simply the market

The fourth bucket is the one that matters. The larger its share, the more of the return came from the market rather than from judgement.

## 8. Mistake journal

Every closed position gets a post-mortem answering two independent questions: **was the outcome right, and was the process right?**

| | Process right | Process wrong |
|---|---|---|
| **Outcome right** | Skill — repeatable | Luck — the most dangerous cell |
| **Outcome wrong** | Bad luck — do not change the method | Deserved — change the method |

Only the bottom row justifies changing how I work. The top-right cell is the only one that manufactures false confidence.
